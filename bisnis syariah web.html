The Wadiah Yad Dhamanah Framework

SAKU blends the simplicity of modern digital banking with the values of Islamic financial principles.

Safe, guaranteed balance

With the Wadiah Yad Dhamanah contract, users entrust their funds to SAKU. The deposited balance remains guaranteed and can be returned whenever users need it.

SAKU may use pooled funds for productive, vetted, and Sharia-compliant activities, while keeping user access simple and transparent.

Hibah, not fixed interest

SAKU does not promise fixed returns. Any bonus is positioned as voluntary Hibah, which means it is not stated, guaranteed, or fixed in advance.

This keeps the savings concept free from riba while still allowing users to enjoy ethical rewards when the platform performs well.

Is SAKU promising a fixed return?

No. SAKU avoids fixed interest. Any bonus is treated as voluntary Hibah and is not promised in advance.

Can users withdraw anytime?

Yes. Under the Wadiah savings concept, users should be able to access their deposited balance whenever needed.

What makes it different from regular banking?

SAKU focuses on zero admin fees, low entry deposit, goal tracking, streaks, and transparent Sharia positioning.

Who supervises the Sharia compliance?

The concept includes supervision by a Sharia Supervisory Board, also known as DPS, to maintain Sharia clarity.
